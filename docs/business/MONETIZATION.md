# Lumet free-first launch and monetization hypothesis

Status: Draft; no payment launch authorized.

## MVP and beta
All users can freely complete the full learning loop: choose technology, read code, predict outcomes, replay a verified trace, submit a review, and read explanations. Do not impose artificial daily caps, forced ads, or paywalls on this core loop.

## Measurement
With appropriate consent and privacy controls, validate lesson start/completion, repeat usage, stack requests, qualitative learning outcomes, feature requests, and willingness to pay. Set thresholds after the first beta baseline; avoid fabricating conversion targets.

## Future optional offers (NOT in MVP)
Advanced scenarios, downloadable learning packs, personalized paths, and cost-incurring AI or isolated own-code analysis subject to transparent usage limits. Keep existing free core features free. Validate pricing, payment regulations, unit economics, and rights to paid content before launch.
